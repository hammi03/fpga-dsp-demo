8-tap Hamming-windowed sinc, cut-off 0.25 × fs. Coefficients are scaled integers (×256). DC gain = 1.0 exactly. H(Nyquist) = 0 exactly.

Design rationale
Why Hamming window? Hamming tapering suppresses stopband sidelobes while keeping the filter short enough for 8 taps. Scaling coefficients by ×256 keeps all multiply-accumulate arithmetic in 32-bit integers — no floating-point hardware. DC gain and Nyquist rejection are verified exact (not approximate) in the testbench.

Design rationale
Why a matched filter? For a known pulse shape, the matched filter maximises output SNR at the moment of alignment — no other causal linear filter does better. The theoretical peak equals the signal's energy divided by the scale factor (380 here). The threshold is set at 50 % of this peak, giving a clear margin over mismatched signals.
